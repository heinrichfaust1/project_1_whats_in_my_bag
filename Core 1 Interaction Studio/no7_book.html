Description
A tragic play in two parts by the german renaissance man Goethe, a desert island book for me, in which that I had to keep, carry, and re-read, thus belongs to my bag. When times get rough, times of struggle and fail, reading this book has always led me to greater ambition. The themes of restless, unswerving human will and salvation is such a powerful motivator.

Memories
It has been a favorite novel since I was young. I've returned to it at different points in my life. Got to own this edition as a gift from my father for this new school year.

Dreams
I shall understand Faust deeper as I get older.

Wonders
I wonder if my perspective and values would change, of will, ambition, and spirit, in the future. If so, I wonder how I will interpret Faust differently.
